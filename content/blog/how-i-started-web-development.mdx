## The Beginning

My journey into web development started during my first year at university. I was fascinated by the idea of building things that could be used by people around the world, and I wanted to understand how websites worked behind the scenes.

I started by learning the basics: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It wasn't easy at first, and I made a lot of mistakes. But every mistake taught me something valuable.

## Resources That Helped

Here are some resources that made a huge difference in my learning:

1. **FreeCodeCamp** - Great interactive tutorials
2. **MDN Web Docs** - The most comprehensive reference
3. **Building Small Projects** - The best way to learn

## Common Mistakes I Made

- Trying to learn everything at once
- Not building enough projects
- Giving up too quickly when things got hard

## My Advice

Start small, build something, and keep learning. The web development community is incredibly supportive, and there are resources everywhere.
